## Contents 
1. Adam Newman (2) - Rocket Scientist who illustrates repression and growing maturity
2. Dr. Godfrey Camille (5) - Lonely physician who spent his life finding love, lifelong personality development and the ability to let love in.
3. Art Miller (0) - Elusive high combat veteran and drama professor who found peace in Australia
4. Two cases - 
   1. Oliver Holmes (6) - Judge from an idyllic childhood who illustrates its gift to old age. 
   2. Sam Lovelace (0) - Architect with a miserable childhood who illustrates its lasting curse. 
   3. Algernon Young (0) - Blue collar worker from an aristocratic family - an illustration of development derailed. 
5. Five cases - 
   1. Charles Boatwright (6) - Optimistic boatyard owner; a model of empathy 
   2. Prof. Peter Penn (1) - College professor. A man who never quite grew up. 
   3. Prof. George Bancroft (7) - College professor who illustrates the sequential steps to maturity. 
   4. Dr. Eric Carey (TY) - Polio victim who achieved Eriksonian integrity in the face of an early death. 
   5. Dr. Carlton Tarryton (TY) -  A physician who failed to reach career consolidation. 
6. Four cases - 
   1. John Adams (6) - A lawyer with three divorces and a happy long fourth marriage.
   2. Fredrick Chipp (7) - A school teacher from a warm childhoood who created a lifelong happy family. 
   3. Dr. Carlton Tarryton (TY) - A physician with four unhappy marriages. 
   4. Eben Frost (7) - A mentally healthy lawyer who lacked the gift or desire for intimacy. 
7. two cases - 
   1. Daniel Garrick (3) - An actor , a late bloomer, who illustrtes the importance of old age of good (subjective and objective ) physical health.
   2. Alfred Paine (0) - A manager with no ability to let love in. he illustrates aging in the absence of  good health. 
8. 2 cases 
   1. Dylan Bright (TY): An English professor gifted in sublimation. 
   2. Francis DeMille (2): A businessman. He illustrates personality development from repression and dissociation to maturity. 
9. 2 cases
   1.  James O Neill (TY) - An economics professor who illustrates the destructive power of alcoholic denial. 
   2.  Bill Loman (2) - Francis Lowell - An aristocratic lawyer who provides simultaneous illustrations of alcoholism as voluntary career and alcoholism as debilitating disease. 
10. Two cases - 
    1.  Bert Hoover (TY) - A study conservative  
    2.  Oscar Weil (TY) - A study liberal 
11. Prof Ernest Clovis (3) - A french professor who illustrates both sublimation and guardianship
